Recovery Timeline

Understanding your post-operative journey is crucial for optimal results. Dr. Bhushan’s clinical team provides 24/7 support throughout this process.

"The success of a transplant depends as much on post-care as the procedure itself."

signature
Dr. Bhushan

First 48 Hours

Initial healing phase. Minor redness and swelling are normal. Patients are provided with a specialized saline spray for graft hydration.

Day 7 - 10

Grafts are fully anchored. Most redness has subsided. First PRP follow-up treatment is scheduled to boost healing factors.

Month 1 - 3

"The Shedding Phase." Transplanted hairs fall out as follicles enter a new growth cycle. This is an expected part of the biological process.

Month 6 - 12

New, healthy hair begins to emerge. Texture thickens monthly. Final aesthetic results are typically visible between months 10 and 12.

Lifelong Maintenance

Quarterly PRP sessions are recommended to maintain the health of surrounding non-transplanted follicles.
